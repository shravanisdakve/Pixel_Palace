How to Play: Brick Smash Chronicles

Objective:

Clear all bricks from the screen by hitting them with the ball to advance to the next level.

Controls:

  • Mouse: Move the paddle horizontally to bounce the ball.
  • Spacebar (Optional): Launch the ball at the start of a round.

Rules & Tips:

  • Don't let the ball fall off the bottom of the screen – you'll lose a life!
  • Hitting bricks multiple times may be required to destroy them.
  • Some bricks might contain power-ups or special effects – catch them!

How to Play: Hare's High-Speed Chase

Objective:

Be the first player to reach a score of 100 points. Players take turns rolling a die and accumulating points.

Controls:

  • "ROLL DICE" Button: Roll the die to get points.
  • "HOLD" Button: Bank your current turn's points and pass to the next player.

Rules & Tips:

  • If you roll a '1', you lose all points accumulated in that turn and your turn ends.
  • You can roll as many times as you like, but beware of rolling a '1'.
  • The first player to reach 100 or more points wins.
