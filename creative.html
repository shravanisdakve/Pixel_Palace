How to Use: SketchCraft Studio

Objective:

Create digital drawings, sketches, and paintings using various tools and colors.

Controls:

  • Mouse Left Click & Drag: Draw on the canvas.
  • Color Picker: Select different colors for your brush.
  • Brush Size Slider: Adjust the thickness of your brush strokes.

Tips:

  • Experiment with different colors and brush sizes to create unique artwork.
  • Use the eraser tool to correct mistakes or create interesting effects.
  • Don't be afraid to try new things and let your imagination guide you!

How to Use: Digital Whiteboard Canvas

Objective:

Utilize a digital canvas for drawing, writing, and sharing ideas freely.

Controls:

  • Mouse Left Click & Drag: Draw on the whiteboard.
  • Tool Palette: Select different drawing tools (pen, eraser), colors, and line thicknesses.
  • Clear Button: Erase everything on the whiteboard.

Tips:

  • This tool is great for quick sketches, brainstorming sessions, or explaining concepts visually.
  • Experiment with various colors and brush sizes to make your ideas stand out.

How to Play: Virtual Piano Symphony

Objective:

Play musical notes and create melodies using a virtual piano keyboard.

Controls:

  • Keyboard Keys: Press corresponding keyboard keys to play piano notes.
  • Mouse Click: Click on the piano keys directly.

Tips:

  • Familiarize yourself with which keyboard keys correspond to which piano notes.
  • Practice scales and chords to improve your virtual piano skills.
  • Experiment with different tempos and rhythms to compose your own music.
