How to Play: Typing Sprint Arena

Objective:

Type the provided text accurately and quickly to achieve a high WPM (Words Per Minute) score.

Controls:

  • Keyboard: Type the displayed text.
  • "Start" Button: Begin the typing test.

Rules & Tips:

  • Focus on accuracy first, then speed. Mistakes can slow you down.
  • Keep your eyes on the screen, not your keyboard.
  • Practice regularly to see significant improvements in your typing speed.

How to Play: Wordmaster Challenge

Objective:

Form as many words as possible from a given set of scrambled letters within a time limit.

Controls:

  • Keyboard: Type in words you find.
  • "Submit" Button: Enter your guessed word.

Rules & Tips:

  • Words must typically be a minimum length (e.g., 3 letters).
  • You cannot use the same letter more times than it appears in the scrambled set.
  • Longer and more complex words often yield higher scores.
  • Look for common prefixes, suffixes, and letter combinations.

How to Play: Number Cruncher Quest

Objective:

Answer a series of arithmetic questions correctly within a time limit to achieve the highest score.
