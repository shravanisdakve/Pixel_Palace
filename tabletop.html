Play against the dealer in this classic card game where the goal is to get 21!

How to Play: Blackjack Royale

Objective:

Beat the dealer by having a hand value higher than the dealer's without exceeding 21. Aim for Blackjack (an Ace and a 10-value card).

Controls:

  • "Hit" Button: Draw another card.
  • "Stand" Button: End your turn and keep your current hand.
  • "Deal" Button: Start a new round.

Rules & Tips:

  • Cards 2 through 10 are worth their face value.
  • Face cards (King, Queen, Jack) are worth 10.
  • Aces can be worth 1 or 11, whichever benefits your hand more.
  • If your hand exceeds 21, you "bust" and lose.
  • The dealer must hit until their hand is 17 or higher.

AI Tic-Tac Duel

Play the classic game of Tic-Tac-Toe against a smart AI opponent or a friend!

Play
×

How to Play: AI Tic-Tac Duel

Objective:

Be the first to get three of your marks in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) on a 3x3 grid.

Controls:

  • Mouse Click: Click on an empty square to place your mark (X or O).

Rules & Tips:

  • You can choose to play against an AI opponent or another player.
  • The game ends when one player gets three in a row or the board is full (a draw).
  • Try to block your opponent while simultaneously creating your own winning lines.

How to Play: Picture Bingo Blast

Objective:

Be the first player to match a line of five pictures (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) on your bingo card.

Controls:

  • Mouse Click: Click on a picture on your card when it is called out or displayed.

Rules & Tips:

  • Each player receives a unique bingo card with different pictures.
  • Pictures are drawn one by one, and players mark them on their cards.
  • The first player to get Bingo shouts "Bingo!" to win the round.
